🐍 MicroPython web installer


Update your robot to the latest firmware.

Firmware version 1.26.1 | publish date: 2025.09.11

  1. Connect your robot to an USB port in your computer and make sure robot switch is on.
  2. Click on the button below "CONNECT"
    3. The demo is not available because your browser does not support Web Serial. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge instead (but not on your iOS device).
  3. Select "USB-Serial (COM#)"
  4. Click on "INSTALL MICROPYTHON"
  5. After this is completed you must 📚 Update Libraries back in words code
